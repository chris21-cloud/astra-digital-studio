Good questions

Before we get started.

How much does a website cost?

The price depends on the pages, features, and content you need. ASTRA will confirm the scope and price with you before any work begins.

Can I see a sample first?

Yes. Browse the concepts above to see possible design directions. They are examples, not official websites for the named businesses.

Will it work on a phone?

Mobile-friendly design is a core goal, so the layout is planned to be easy to browse on smaller screens as well as computers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Explain what your business does and what you want customers to do. We can discuss the simplest approach together.

When do I pay?

We first discuss the project, deliverables, timing, and price. Any payment terms should be agreed clearly before work starts.

Are the portfolio concepts real client projects?

They are independent sample concepts unless clearly stated otherwise. They are not commissioned or endorsed by the named businesses, and real business details need owner confirmation.
